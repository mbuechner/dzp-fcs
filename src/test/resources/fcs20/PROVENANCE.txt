Resource.xsd permits an empty ResourceFragment, while the normative FCS profile
requires HITS for a normal hit record. DataView-Hits.xsd requires at least one
Hit element; its xs:string type does not itself forbid an empty Hit. Regression
tests therefore cover both XSD validation and these additional profile rules.
